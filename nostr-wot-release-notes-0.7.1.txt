Nostr WoT 0.7.1

- Fix QR and bunker-link onboarding for remote signers that use a connection key different from the user's Nostr identity. The extension now asks the signer for the actual user public key before saving the account.
- Preserve the signer's selected relays, pairing credentials and client identity for subsequent signing and reconnection.
- Reject invalid remote identities, bound connection waits and close temporary signer subscriptions after success or failure.
- Expand integration tests for separate/shared keys, QR relay fallback, vault persistence, signing after linking, authentication challenges, rejection and timeouts.

Previously misidentified remote accounts need to be reconnected. Native delivery of signing requests in Amber remains unverified; this release does not claim to resolve the reported missing-request issue. Android NIP-55, legacy NIP-04 transport and provider discovery are outside this fix.
