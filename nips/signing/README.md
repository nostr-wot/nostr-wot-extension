# Signing proposals

Status: experimental, unnumbered drafts. They describe the same-device signing contract the Nostr WoT wallet implements on iOS and the client library that speaks it; they do not claim adoption by other signers or approval as NIPs.

1. [Same-device signing on iOS](01-ios-same-device-signing.md): the share-sheet transport, NIP-55's methods in batches, App Attest caller identity, the connect link and signing sessions over loopback.
